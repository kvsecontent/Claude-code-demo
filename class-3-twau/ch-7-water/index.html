Teacher guide

Twelve stops follow Chapter 7 "Water – A Precious Gift" (pp. 86–99) in order, in four stretches: Here Comes the Rain, Where Does Rainwater Go?, Water at Home, and Every Drop Counts. Children earn up to 3 drops per stop for first-try answers. Questions shuffle each time, so stops can be replayed.

StopBook pageChildren will…

As the book advises: the water cycle is introduced only in Class 4, so these games only ask children to notice that water on hard surfaces dries up in the sun, and that rainwater soaking into the soil fills our wells. Pair the games with the real activities: watching the rain, visiting a nearby water source, the vessel exhibition and making a birdbath.
