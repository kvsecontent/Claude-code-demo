For teachers: how to use this in class
  • Tilt Detective: compare weights and predict the tilt. Children say their prediction aloud before tapping.
  • Balance It!: repeated addition and multiplication (5 × 10 g = 50 g). Ask “How many?” before anyone taps.
  • Sweet Shop: Mr Shrinathan’s kaju katli and 1 kg packets. Division as equal sharing; ½ kg and ¼ kg.
  • Guess the Weight: ordering heaviest to lightest, the estimate table from the book, and choosing kg or g.
  • Pour It: capacity in L and ml with Mr Krishna’s perfumed oil, dosing cups and droppers.
Weights of animals, fruits and vegetables are estimates. Follow up with a real pan balance and 1 kg, 500 g and 100 g weights, as the textbook suggests.
Based on NCERT Maths Mela, Class 4, Chapter 8 “Weigh It, Pour It”. 1000 g = 1 kg · 1000 ml = 1 L
