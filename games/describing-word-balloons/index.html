Describing Word Balloons

Balloons are floating up into the sky. Each one carries a word. Pop only the describing words before they float away!

What is a describing word?
A describing word (an adjective) tells us more about a naming word (a noun). It can tell us what colour, what size, what shape, how it feels, how it tastes, how it sounds or how many.
a red kite  ·  a tiny ant  ·  a loud drum  ·  five mangoes

Notes for teachers
  • Learning goal: identify adjectives and tell them apart from nouns and verbs; choose an adjective that fits a noun; group adjectives by what they describe.
  • Whole class: put the game on the projector. Students call out "Pop!" or "Leave it!" before you tap. Ask "What does it describe?" after each pop.
  • Mistakes teach: a wrong pop shows why the word is not a describing word (for example, "jump is an action word").
  • After the game: the end screen lists every describing word found. Ask students to use three of them in their own sentences.
  • Differentiate: use Gentle speed for Grade 3 and Zippy speed for Grade 5.
